# Clinical Trial Navigator  
## AI-Assisted Trial Matching

**Clinical Trial Navigator** uses advanced AI and Apple HealthKit integration to help you find clinical trials that match your unique health profile. Whether you’re managing a chronic condition, exploring treatment options, or just curious about research opportunities, our app simplifies the search and brings tailored, up-to-date clinical trial options directly to you.

---

## 🔑 Key Features

- **Personalized Search**  
  Get matched to clinical trials based on your health history, conditions, and preferences.

- **AI-Powered Summaries**  
  Understand complex medical studies quickly with concise, plain-language trial summaries.

- **HealthKit Integration**  
  Seamlessly sync your Apple Health data to enhance trial recommendations.

- **Save & Share**  
  Bookmark your favorite trials and share them with your doctor or loved ones.

- **Always Up-to-Date**  
  Stay informed with real-time updates from official clinical trial databases.
